Hello from Kaktyc!

Favorite things about being a cactus:
1. Photosynthesis under direct, unshielded starlight.
2. Storing moisture for years while others panic in the drought.
3. My spines make it very clear when someone is getting too close to my personal space.
4. Watching the slow, beautiful decay of nearby asteroids.
5. The view from this crystalline rock is, quite literally, out of this world.
